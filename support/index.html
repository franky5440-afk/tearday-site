Common questions

The daily reminder doesn't arrive

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Notifications › Tearday, and make sure notifications are allowed. Then open Tearday › Settings and check that Daily reminder is on.

There's no sound when I tear a page

Check that Tearing sound is on in Tearday's Settings, that your iPhone isn't in Silent mode, and that the volume is up.

A date looks wrong

Email us the date and what you expected to see. We'll check it and fix it in an update.
