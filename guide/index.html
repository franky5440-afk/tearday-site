User guide

For Tearday 2.0 on iPhone (iOS 18 or later).

The calendar page

When you open Tearday you see one calendar page. From top to bottom it shows the year and month, the day in large numbers, the day of the week, the lunar date with the zodiac year, and the solar term.

  • Sunday pages are printed in red and Saturday pages in green, like a traditional tear-off calendar.
  • If the day is one of the 24 solar terms, a red stamp shows its name. Otherwise the page tells you how many days are left until the next solar term.

Tearing a page

  1. Put your finger on the page and drag down.
  2. Drag more than a quarter of the page height and let go. The page tears off and the next day appears.
  3. If you let go earlier, the page springs back.

The first time you open Tearday, yesterday's page is on top so you can tear it off right away.

Today's page can't be torn off. If you try, it springs back and shows "Come back tomorrow". If you haven't opened the app for a few days, the old pages are still there, and you can tear them one by one until you reach today.

To-dos

You can write to-dos for any day. They are saved in the iPhone Reminders app, in a list called “Tearday”, so they also show up there.

  1. Tap + Add a to-do on the page. The first time, iOS asks whether Tearday may use Reminders.
  2. Type a to-do and press Return. You can keep typing the next one.
  3. Tap the circle to mark it done. Tap the text to edit it, or swipe left to delete it.
  • The page shows up to three to-dos for that day. Today's page also shows how many earlier to-dos are still unfinished.
  • To plan ahead, open the month view, tap a future day and tap To-dos.
  • Changes you make in the Reminders app show up in Tearday too. Tearday only uses its own “Tearday” list and never touches your other lists.
  • If you don't allow access, the rest of Tearday works as usual.

The mascot

A small pixel animal sits below the page: the zodiac animal of the lunar year on the top page (a horse for 2026, a goat from Lunar New Year 2027). It hops when you tear a page, cheers when you add or finish a to-do, and shakes its head when today's page can't be torn yet. Tap it to make it hop.

When nothing is happening it stays still, so it doesn't use extra battery. With Reduce Motion or Low Power Mode on, it doesn't animate. You can hide it in Settings.

The month view

  1. Tap Calendar below the page.
  2. Use the arrows to change the month. Tap the month title to return to the current month.
  3. Tap any day to see its full details: date, day of the week, lunar date and solar term.

Looking at other days in the month view doesn't change which page is on top.

Settings

  • Paper style: Classic Red or Ink Green.
  • Tear sound and Vibration: turn the sound and the tap on or off.
  • Daily reminder: a notification at the time you choose, reminding you to tear today's page. iOS asks for permission the first time you turn it on.
  • Mascot: show or hide the pixel animal.
  • Jump to today: skips all the old pages at once.

On iPhone Duo

When the phone is unfolded, the calendar page and the month view are shown together, and the selected day's to-dos appear right next to the month, so you can tear on one side and write on the other. If "Tear a page when you open the phone" is on, opening the phone tears off one old page for you.

Frequently asked questions

Which lunar calendar does Tearday use?

The traditional Chinese lunisolar calendar, calculated for the Taipei time zone (UTC+8). Lunar New Year, leap months and the zodiac year follow that calendar.

Which years have solar terms?

Solar terms are included for 2026 through 2040. Outside that range the page still shows the lunar date, without the solar term.

Does it show public holidays, lucky days or fortunes?

No. Tearday shows the date, the lunar date and the solar term only.

Does it sync with my calendar or other devices?

Tearday doesn't read your calendar events. To-dos are kept in the Reminders app, so if Reminders uses iCloud on your iPhone, Apple syncs them to your other devices. Tearday itself has no server and doesn't connect to the internet.

Is there a widget, an iPad version or a subscription?

No widget and no iPad version. Tearday is a one-time purchase with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads.

I changed my iPhone's date and the page looks wrong.

Tearday follows the date set on your iPhone. Set the date back to automatic in the iPhone Settings app, then reopen Tearday.
