User guide

For Tearday 1.0 on iPhone (iOS 18 or later).

The calendar page

When you open Tearday you see one calendar page. From top to bottom it shows the year and month, the day in large numbers, the day of the week, the lunar date with the zodiac year, and the solar term.

  • Sunday pages are printed in red and Saturday pages in green, like a traditional tear-off calendar.
  • If the day is one of the 24 solar terms, a red stamp shows its name. Otherwise the page tells you how many days are left until the next solar term.

Tearing a page

  1. Put your finger on the page and drag down.
  2. Drag more than a quarter of the page height and let go. The page tears off and the next day appears.
  3. If you let go earlier, the page springs back.

Today's page can't be torn off. If you try, it springs back and shows "Come back tomorrow". If you haven't opened the app for a few days, the old pages are still there, and you can tear them one by one until you reach today.

The month view

  1. Tap Calendar below the page.
  2. Use the arrows to change the month. Tap the month title to return to the current month.
  3. Tap any day to see its full details: date, day of the week, lunar date and solar term.

Looking at other days in the month view doesn't change which page is on top.

Settings

  • Paper style: Classic Red or Ink Green.
  • Tearing sound and Haptics: turn the sound and the tap on or off.
  • Daily reminder: a notification at the time you choose, reminding you to tear today's page. iOS asks for permission the first time you turn it on.
  • Jump to today: skips all the old pages at once.

On iPhone Duo

When the phone is unfolded, the calendar page and the month view are shown together. If "Tear a page when the phone opens" is on, opening the phone tears off one old page for you.

Frequently asked questions

Which lunar calendar does Tearday use?

The traditional Chinese lunisolar calendar, calculated for the Taipei time zone (UTC+8). Lunar New Year, leap months and the zodiac year follow that calendar.

Which years have solar terms?

Solar terms are included for 2026 through 2040. Outside that range the page still shows the lunar date, without the solar term.

Does it show public holidays, lucky days or fortunes?

No. Tearday shows the date, the lunar date and the solar term only.

Does it sync with my calendar or other devices?

No. Tearday doesn't read your calendar events and doesn't sync. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Is there a widget, an iPad version or a subscription?

No widget and no iPad version in 1.0. Tearday is a one-time purchase with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads.

I changed my iPhone's date and the page looks wrong.

Tearday follows the date set on your iPhone. Set the date back to automatic in the iPhone Settings app, then reopen Tearday.
