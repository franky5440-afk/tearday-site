Tearday

A tear-off desk calendar for iPhone, with lunar dates and the 24 solar terms.

Stand your iPhone on the desk and it becomes an old-fashioned daily calendar. Tear off one page a day to see today's date, the lunar date, and the solar term.

  • One page a day. Drag the page down to tear it off, with a paper sound and a tap you can feel.
  • Lunar dates and solar terms. Each page shows the lunar month and day, the zodiac year, and the solar term, or how many days until the next one.
  • Nothing else. No ads, no account, no internet connection, no data collection.

App Store: Download Tearday on the App Store (US$2.99, one-time purchase).
