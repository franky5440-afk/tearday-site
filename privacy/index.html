Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-15

The short version

Tearday doesn't collect any data. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no third-party code, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

What stays on your iPhone

Tearday stores a few settings on your iPhone: which page is on top, your paper style, and your sound, haptics and reminder choices. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the reminder is scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. No server is involved.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details.

Children

Tearday doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
